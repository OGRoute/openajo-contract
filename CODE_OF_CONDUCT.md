# Code of Conduct

OpenAjo is built for people who save together on trust. We hold the project to
the same standard: contributors of every background, experience level and
language are welcome, and everyone is expected to make that true for others.

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially with first-time contributors
  and people writing in a second language.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific about what to change.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain why when they do.
- Credit other people's work, including issue reports and reviews.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including wallet-to-identity links,
  without their explicit permission.
- Claiming issues you do not intend to work on in order to block others.
- Submitting plagiarised work, or unreviewed machine-generated changes, as your
  own.

## Scope

This applies in all OGRoute repositories — issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions — and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer, **@adelekevictor12**. Mention them
on any issue asking for a private channel and they will contact you; you do
not need to describe the problem publicly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or block contributors who
break this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines:
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
